title: "Fixes transcription going silent after a reload"
date: "2026-09-09"
---

A fix for 0.3.0's transcription feature. Reloading a saved session, or a host
changing buffer size or sample rate mid-session, could leave transcription
silently dead until the plugin was reloaded a second time.

**The cause was a race inside the transcription engine.** A pending reset
could publish a freshly analysed block's notes into the very window it had
just asked to clear, discarding them before anything read them — and a
discarded note-start can't be recovered, so the picture stopped sounding.
Fixed by waiting for the reset to finish before publishing anything into it.

No parameter, saved session, or rendered audio changes.
