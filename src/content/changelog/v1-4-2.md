A small follow-up to 1.4.1 that closes the last two open bugs.

**The score shows every register.** The pitch axis was hard-coded to A0–C8
while the engine, since 1.4.0's register controls, produces C0–B8 — notes at
the extremes were invisible. The axis is now derived from the engine's own
register constants.

**A retrigger restarts a sounding note** instead of choking it: `MidiOut` now
closes any still-sounding same-note before the new note-on, and the built-in
synth fades the old slot in step so both halves restart together.

Test suite: engine 100 → 106 cases, plugin 112 → 115.
