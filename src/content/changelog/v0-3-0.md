title: "Spectral transcription: the picture leaves the plugin as MIDI"
date: "2026-09-09"
---

A peak tracker follows the partials in the spectrum Ekphrasis is already
rendering and emits each one as a note, so another instrument can play the
image. Off by default, behind a new **Transcribe** toggle.

**MPE encoding carries the frequency exactly.** Master channel 1, one member
channel per live partial, and each note's precise pitch riding a per-note
pitch bend rather than snapping to the nearest semitone — a partial at
441.3 Hz arrives as 441.3 Hz, and a glide bends continuously instead of
retriggering at every semitone. Long glides still fracture into several bent
notes as the tracker loses and reacquires a partial; the bend removes the
stair-step, not the fracturing.

**Two new controls.** `Transcribe Voices` (1–8) caps how many notes sound at
once; `Transcribe Threshold` (0.01–0.90) sets how faint a partial can be and
still emit, measured against the image's raw magnitudes so one knob position
means the same thing on a bright picture and a dark one.

**MIDI Thru changes shape.** With transcription on, the incoming MIDI stream
is now captured and replayed around Ekphrasis's own notes rather than left
alone; with transcription off, nothing changes. SysEx does not survive that
path — one to three bytes still do, which covers every channel message and
the realtime stream (clock, start/stop, active sensing).

Reachable in Live from the VST3 only — Live exposes a VST3's MIDI output as a
routing source and never an AU's. A 0.2.0 session sounds exactly as it did
with transcription switched on: a golden-audio test renders the identity take
with and without it and asserts the two are byte-identical.

Not included: Logic support (needs an AU MIDI FX build), Windows and Linux
builds, polyphony, an in-plugin update check.
