First release since 1.1.0 and 1.1.1: long-form sync, a legible score at every
zoom, two long-standing gaps in getting MIDI out, and per-voice register
control.

**Sync units from 2 to 64 bars.** Sync mode previously topped out at 1
bar — two seconds at 120 BPM. The range now goes to 128 seconds per step.

**Per-voice register range.** Each voice can be confined to its own octaves
as a remap of the register series rather than a clamp, so a narrowed voice
still traces the same gesture, compressed.

**Save MIDI file.** A Save button beside the drag chip — the only way to get
MIDI out of Seriatim in Logic, where a software-instrument channel strip has
no MIDI output slot.

**Clear the score**, and **a legible score at maximum zoom**: the span is now
bounded by how many events would actually be on screen.

Please read before updating: existing Sync Unit automation is remapped by
growing the choice list from 11 to 17 entries — a lane written at full scale
now resolves to 64 bars instead of 1. Projects without Sync Unit automation
are unaffected. This release has not been verified in a DAW.
